Synthetic potholes for Irish weather BT Young Scientist 2025

Most pothole detectors are trained on clear, sunny images, so they struggle in rain, fog and damp, which is most of the time in Ireland. I used generative AI (supervised and unsupervised image-to-image translation) to create synthetic images of potholes in Irish weather conditions, and tested whether training on them makes detection more reliable.
